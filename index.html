Our Core Manifesto

  • Privacy by Default: Zero telemetry, zero hidden trackers, and zero third-party data harvesting.
  • Zero AI Snooping: Your personal notes, files, and credentials are never scanned or used to train external AI models.
  • Built for Every Indian Device: Engineered to run blazingly fast even on older 4GB RAM student laptops.
  • 100% Open-Source Transparency: Publicly auditable code built collaboratively for Bharat.
